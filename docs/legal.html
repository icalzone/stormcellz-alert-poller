Privacy Policy

Information we collect

  • Location. StormCellz uses your device's location (or a location you search for) to fetch weather data for that area. Saved locations are stored on your device.
  • Push notification data. If you enable severe weather alerts, we register your device with Firebase Cloud Messaging and associate it with the locations you've chosen to receive alerts for, so we can deliver relevant notifications.
  • Access codes. If you unlock friends or developer access, the app stores that unlock state on your device. We do not collect any payment or account information — StormCellz has no subscriptions.

How we use it

Location data is sent to our weather data providers (see the Weather Data Disclaimer below) solely to retrieve forecasts, observations, and alerts for that location. Push notification registration is used only to deliver the severe weather alerts you've opted into.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data. We do not share your location or usage data with advertisers.
