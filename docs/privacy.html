Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026

StormCellz is a weather app built for people who want fast, accurate forecasts without giving up their data in exchange. This page explains exactly what the app collects, why, and what it never does.

No account required

StormCellz does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal identifying information to use the app.

Location data

When you use your current location or search for and save a location, StormCellz sends that location's coordinates to weather data providers (the National Weather Service, Apple WeatherKit, and OpenWeather) solely to retrieve a forecast for that spot. Saved locations are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to or stored on any StormCellz server.

Push notifications

If you enable severe weather alerts, your device registers a push token with Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), a Google service, and subscribes to topic-based alert channels relevant to your saved locations. This token is used only to deliver alert notifications to your device — StormCellz does not maintain user profiles, accounts, or a database of personal information tied to this token.

Personal weather stations

If you choose to connect a Tempest personal weather station, StormCellz communicates directly with the Tempest API using credentials you provide, to display your station's observations within the app. This is entirely opt-in.

What StormCellz does not do

  • No third-party advertising or ad-tracking SDKs
  • No sale of personal data to any third party
  • No analytics data is currently collected — analytics infrastructure exists in the app but is inactive
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking

Third-party services used

StormCellz relies on the following third-party services to function, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) — U.S. government, no API key or personal data required
  • Apple WeatherKit — governed by Apple's privacy policy
  • OpenWeather API — supplementary forecast data
  • Tempest API — only if you connect a personal weather station
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — only if you enable push notifications

Children's privacy

StormCellz is not directed at children under 13, and the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
